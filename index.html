Getting around

This is the drone survey of the site, with the steel frame model of the car park in its place. The car park is split-level: each level is half a storey above the last, alternating between the north-west (NW) and south-east (SE) decks. Pick a level on the left. Use Orbit to fly around the outside, or Walk to look around at eye height. Click the small plan to jump to a spot.
